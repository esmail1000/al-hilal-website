## Design Direction

Primary Style:
Premium Industrial + Architectural Minimalism

Supporting Style:
Swiss Corporate / Editorial Architecture

The website should feel like a major building materials manufacturer,
not an e-commerce store, SaaS dashboard, or luxury fashion brand.

Use strong geometry, clean grids, large product photography,
technical specifications, controlled white space, and confident typography.

---

## Visual Principles

1. Large industrial photography
2. Strong architectural grid
3. Minimal decorative elements
4. Clear technical information
5. Strong hierarchy
6. Premium but practical appearance
7. Product-first presentation
8. High readability
9. Generous whitespace
10. Subtle motion only

## Motion

Use motion sparingly.

Allowed:

- Fade-up
- Subtle reveal
- Image scale on hover
- Underline transitions
- Number animations
- Section transitions

Timing:
150-500ms

Avoid:

- Constant movement
- Excessive parallax
- Floating objects
- 3D gimmicks
- Large cursor effects

Respect:
prefers-reduced-motion
